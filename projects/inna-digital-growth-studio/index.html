D2C Skincare Brand

Challenge

Marketing ran on six disconnected tools. No one could say which channel actually drove revenue.

Analysis

Mapped the full customer journey and found the real bottleneck: reporting, not ad spend.

Solution

Unified analytics, rebuilt the funnel, automated weekly reporting with an AI summary layer.

Result

Decisions made in days, not weeks — and a clear view of what to cut.

B2B SaaS Platform

Challenge

Every new hire meant a new undocumented workaround. Projects slipped without anyone noticing.

Analysis

Found the gap wasn't talent — it was the absence of a shared operating structure.

Solution

Designed a project management system with clear ownership and automated status tracking.

Result

Deadlines became predictable; onboarding time cut by more than half.

Regional Logistics Company

Challenge

Leadership wanted "more AI" but couldn't name a single process worth automating.

Analysis

Audited daily operations and found three repetitive, high-volume manual tasks.

Solution

Automated those three tasks only — no platform overhaul, no new headcount.

Result

Twelve hours a week returned to the team, with zero added complexity.
